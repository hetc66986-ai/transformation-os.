How to progress

  • Start with the lower end of each rep range and leave 2-3 good reps in reserve.
  • When every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 1-2 reps or a little backpack weight.
  • For one-sided exercises, complete both sides.
  • Keep a note of reps and backpack load so you can beat your previous performance gradually.

For the first two weeks, prioritise technique and consistency over intensity.

Vegetarian nutrition

At 77 kg, a practical muscle-building protein range is roughly 1.2-1.6 g/kg/day (about 92-123 g). Build toward it with normal meals, not extreme dieting.

Simple vegetarian protein options

Milk, curd/Greek yogurt, paneer, tofu, soy chunks, lentils, chickpeas, beans, and whey if you choose to use it. Distribute protein across meals.

Daily habit checklist

When an urge or boredom hits

  1. Pause for 10 minutes before acting.
  2. Change location and put the phone away.
  3. Do a short replacement action: walk, shower, tidy your desk, or start a 10-minute study block.
  4. If you slip, record the trigger and return to your routine without self-punishment.
